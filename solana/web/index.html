3 Escrows — yours as payer, yours as recipient

Read from the chain every 8 s. Release is open to anyone once the date has passed; before it, the program answers TooEarly. Cancel only exists if a cancel date was written, and only after it. Close deletes a finished record and returns its rent to the payer.

4 Shared vault — a deposit several people decide on, 2 of 3

The rental-deposit shape: the tenant funds it, the landlord is the beneficiary, a mediator is the third voice. Each party votes how much goes to the beneficiary (all, nothing, or a split; the rest returns to the owner). When two votes agree, that very vote pays. Past the deadline with no decision, anyone may return the deposit to the owner.

Parties: you (owner), the beneficiary, the second party.
