dareskro on Solana · mainnet · Intents Connect

The payer signs once in the EVM wallet they already have, with USDC or ETH on Base or Arbitrum. Aurora Intents bridges it to SOL and, from the payer's own intermediary account on Solana, runs dareskro's create: the pay is locked for one recipient, released on the date to that recipient only, and nobody, not even the payer, can take it back or redirect it. No desk holds the money in between. Real money: start with a few dollars.
