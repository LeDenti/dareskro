# BottomRadar — the overbought alert

**Markets do not ring a bell at the top. BottomRadar does.**

BottomRadar watches 77 crypto tokens every ten minutes and computes one number per token: the **BR Score**. It is not a prediction. It is a measurement of how stretched a token is right now, on the same scale for every token, so that "stretched" means the same thing for Bitcoin and for a mid-cap.

## What the alert does

When the BR Score of a token in your watchlist crosses the threshold you chose, you get one message: the token, the score, the price, the time. Nothing else. No chart to open, no newsletter, no "we think". One line you can act on, or ignore.

- **Your threshold, not ours.** Set it at 80 if you want to hear about everything that is hot; at 95 if you only want the extremes.
- **Your watchlist.** The 77 tokens are there; alerts fire only for the ones you follow.
- **No noise.** A token that stays above the threshold does not fire again until it has come back under it. One signal per excursion.

## What it is not

The BR Score does not tell you a token will fall. Overbought tokens stay overbought for weeks in a bull run; we measured it. The alert tells you *where* the token is on the scale, so that the decision — sell a part, tighten a stop, do nothing — is yours, with a number behind it.
