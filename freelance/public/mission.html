How this works, in four sentences

The client locked the money in an escrow of the XRP Ledger: it left the client's balance and sits in the ledger, with your address as the only possible destination. The client cannot take it back before the deadline. A named oracle holds the secret that releases it and reveals that secret on proof: a file with the agreed hash at the agreed URL, or the client's signed acceptance. If no proof comes before the deadline, the ledger refunds the client; nobody, including us, ever holds the money.

You need nothing but your XRPL address. Check everything on the explorer links: they are the ledger, not our word.
