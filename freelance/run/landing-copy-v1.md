# dareskro for freelancers — landing page copy, v1

**Deliverable of the mission "Landing page copy, v1", 31 October 2026.**
Published as a GitHub release asset so that the oracle can read it at a public URL.

## Headline

Your fee is locked before you start. You are paid the moment you deliver.

## Sub-headline

dareskro puts the client's money in an escrow of the XRP Ledger at the order.
Nobody holds it in between: not the client, not us. A named oracle releases it
on proof of delivery; if nothing is delivered by the deadline, the ledger
refunds the client by itself.

## Three lines for the client

1. You lock the fee once, with your own key. It leaves your balance and sits in
   the ledger, with the freelancer's address as the only possible destination.
2. You choose how it is released: on a file whose hash you both agreed, read by
   the oracle at a public URL; or on your signed acceptance, when the work
   cannot be described in advance.
3. If nothing comes by the deadline, you are refunded. No chasing, no dispute
   about the invoice: the ledger settles silence as a refund.

## Three lines for the freelancer

1. You start when the escrow is on the explorer, not when the client promises.
2. You are paid without anyone approving a transfer: a fact pays you, or the
   client's signature does, and you know which before you start.
3. You need an XRPL address. No account, no password, no platform fee on the
   payment itself: the oracle pays the fee of the release, about twelve drops.

## What we say plainly

The acceptance mode has no arbiter. The client cannot take the money back before
the deadline, and the client's silence past the deadline is a refund. You keep
the on-chain proof that the money was locked and that no acceptance was signed.
That is a smaller dispute than "the invoice is unpaid", not a solved one.
