1 · The owner is verified once

  • From the Concordium Wallet, a zero-knowledge proof: age ≥ 18, from a testnet identity provider. No name, no date of birth.
  • The proof answers a fresh challenge whose fingerprint is anchored on chain before the request (register_data, tag DKAVR): it cannot be replayed.
  • The server checks it against the chain (getPublicData + verifyPresentation): same challenge, same statement, credential Active.
  • The proving credential is mapped to its account: it must be the account that owns the agent (3f3W…Ecx3).
  • The result is an owner_verification entry of the Article 12 record, anchored.

On Concordium every account comes from a verified identity; the proof adds a fresh, specific, private claim tied to this agent's owner.
